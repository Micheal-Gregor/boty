🎀 Business of the Year

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Business of the Year is a board game. We collect only what's needed to sign you in, run your games, and take a one-time payment if you choose to host. We don't run ads, we don't sell your data, and you can delete everything at any time.

01 Who we are

Business of the Year ("the game," "we," "us") is operated by ProBookkeeping. You can reach us at support@probookkeeping.net. The game is available on the web at boty.probookkeeping.net and as an app in the Google Play and Microsoft stores.

02 What we collect

  • Your email address — so we can send your one-time sign-in code and recognize your account. There's no password.
  • Gameplay data — the games you're in, your seat, and the moves made, so multiplayer games stay in sync and you can resume them.
  • Feedback you send — if you use the in-app "Send feedback" button, we receive your message and which screen you were on.
  • Purchase status — if you buy the one-time host license or make a donation, we record only that your account is licensed. Your card details are entered on Stripe's payment page and never touch our servers.

We do not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for advertising.

03 How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To run and synchronize your multiplayer games, cover a seat with a stand-in if you drop, and let you resume.
  • To unlock hosting after a purchase, and to answer feedback you send us.
  • To keep the service secure and working (basic diagnostics and abuse prevention).

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we never sell it.

04 Who processes it for us

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run the game. They only process data on our behalf:

ProviderWhat they handle
SupabaseSign-in, and the database that stores your email, games, and feedback.
VercelHosting that serves the game and its pages.
StripePayment processing for the host license and donations. Stripe receives your payment details directly; we do not.

These providers may store data in the United States. We share your information with them only as needed to provide the game, and not with anyone else except where required by law.

05 Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time — in the app under ⚙️ Settings → Delete my account, or by following boty.probookkeeping.net/delete-account.html.

When you delete your account we remove your profile, sign-in, games, and feedback. Stripe may retain basic payment records as required by law for accounting and fraud prevention; those records contain no game data.

06 Cookies and local storage

The game stores a few things on your own device — your sign-in session, your settings, and cached game art so it loads fast. These are functional only. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

07 Children

Business of the Year is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us their information, email support@probookkeeping.net and we will delete it.

08 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. You can delete your data yourself (Section 5) or contact us at support@probookkeeping.net and we'll help.

09 Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, note it in the game. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10 Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email support@probookkeeping.net.
