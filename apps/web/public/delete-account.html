🎀 Business of the Year

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Business of the Year account and all of your data at any time. Here's how — and exactly what gets removed.

01

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Business of the Year and sign in.
  2. Tap ⚙️ Settings (on the main menu, or the gear icon during a game).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap 🗑️ Delete my account.
  4. Confirm with Yes, delete everything. Your account is removed immediately and you're signed out.
02

Or request it by email

Can't get into the app? Email us from the address your account uses and we'll delete it for you, usually within a few days:

support@probookkeeping.net

Please send it from the same email you signed up with, so we can verify the account is yours.

03

What gets deleted

This can't be undone

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile and sign-in (email and login history)
  • Your host license, if you bought one — it is not refundable or transferable
  • Your seat in every game, and any games you were hosting
  • Any feedback you sent from inside the app

We keep no personal profile data after deletion. Basic payment records held by our payment processor (Stripe) may be retained by them as required by law for accounting and fraud prevention; those contain no game data.
