What to try

The interesting stuff is what happens when people come and go mid-game. Each card below is a thing to poke at — and what you should see if it's working.

🎲 Play a full game

TryHost or join a table with a friend (add a CPU or two to fill seats), then play a full year through to the Gala. ExpectTurns pass cleanly between everyone; the game reaches the awards Gala without freezing.

📴 Drop & come back

TryMid-game, one player force-quits the app (or loses signal) for a minute — then reopens it and taps ▶ Resume a game. ExpectA stand-in bot covers their seat so the table keeps moving; when they return, they get their seat back where it left off.

👋 The host leaves

TryHave the host quit mid-game while others are still playing. Then have the host come back and resume it. ExpectThe game saves; the others get a "the host has left" note. When the host returns, everyone gets a Return badge on 👥 Players to jump back in.

🖥️ Two windows, one game

TryOpen the same game in a second tab or device on the same account. ExpectThe older window goes read-only ("this game is open in another window") with a Play here button to move control over.

🔓 Buy your way to hosting

TryOn an account that can't host yet, tap Unlock hosting — $5 and pay with the test card. ExpectYou bounce back to the game and, within a second or two, the Host a game button unlocks on its own — no one has to flip a switch for you.

💬 Tell me what you find

Anything weird, confusing, or just an idea — tap 💬 Send feedback on the main menu, or inside ⚙️ Settings during a game. It comes straight to me, with a note of which screen you were on.

The more specific the better: what you did, what you expected, what actually happened.
