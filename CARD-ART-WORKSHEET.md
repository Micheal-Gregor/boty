# Card art worksheet — flavor + animation prompt

Every card's art goes at **`apps/web/src/assets/art/card/<id>.<ext>`** (still `.webp/.png` or animation `.mp4/.webm`; the animation's first frame is the still). Compose each as **building + character(s) + the prompt** — buildings from the building list, characters from `townsfolk/`. Keep a character's card scenes visually consistent with their `townsfolk/` portrait so players connect them.

**Global style to prepend:** *"Maple Hollow small-town Americana, warm but slightly uncanny (Twin-Peaks-wholesome), painterly; a short looping moment; landscape or square framing."*

Columns: **Card** (filename · name) · **Flavor** (what's on the card — tweak the prompt against this) · **Animation prompt** (starting point).
